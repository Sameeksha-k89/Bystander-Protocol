How It Works

Understanding the philosophy, data flow, and why police aren't directly involved

The Philosophy

Bystander intervention is about civilian coordination, not confrontation or policing. Most people who witness harassment or distress want to help — but they freeze because they don't know what's socially safe to do alone.

How Data is Collected

  • Anonymous — No names, no email, no accounts
  • Local Storage — Reports are saved in your browser
  • Exportable — Download reports as text files
  • No PII collected — No personal information stored

Why Police Aren't Directly Involved

  • Police are for emergencies — Call 112 for immediate danger
  • Civilian first response — Most incidents need human connection, not police
  • Documentation matters — Reports become evidence you can share
  • Privacy first — Some people don't want police involvement
